What we build

Nullform Audio develops audio plugins for macOS and Windows.

PentaGraph is modelled on a real hardware EQ pedal that was measured in the studio. DecayChain is a bit-crusher built around 265 wavetables.

Plugin details

Hardware measurement

PentaGraph's centre frequencies, Q values and gain mapping come from studio measurements of a real 5-band gyrator EQ pedal.

Zero latency

Neither plugin adds latency to the signal chain, and neither allocates heap memory on the audio thread.

Tone shaping

PentaGraph's Q narrows at extreme boost and cut, and its saturation responds to input level. DecayChain's envelope routing modulates sample-rate reduction and bit depth.

Plugin formats

VST3 and AU on macOS, VST3 on Windows. PentaGraph also runs standalone on macOS.

PentaGraph

5-band graphic EQ plugin

A 5-band graphic equaliser modelled on the EQ circuit found in guitar amplifiers and in standalone gyrator pedals. Its response is calibrated from studio measurements of a real pedal, with parallel band summing, gain-dependent Q and soft-clipping saturation.

  • Five fixed bands: 80 Hz · 240 Hz · 750 Hz · 2.2 kHz · 6.6 kHz
  • Parallel summing topology for true band independence
  • Analog-modelled saturation via anti-aliased soft clipping
  • Calibrated from real hardware measurements
  • VST3 & AU on macOS · VST3 on Windows

DecayChain

Bit-crusher & wavetable distortion plugin

A bit-crusher and wavetable distortion plugin with 265 original wavetables, a preamp and tone stage, a bit section that can sit before or after the wavetable mapper, table morph, envelope routing, and two DSP modes (Legacy & ADAA).

  • Bit-crusher with Depth and Mask modes · Pre/Post placement
  • 265 procedurally generated wavetables with hex index & morph
  • Preamp, tilt EQ, sample-rate reducer, envelope routing
  • Dual DSP: Legacy (lo-fi) and ADAA (anti-aliased)
  • VST3 & AU on macOS · VST3 on Windows
DecayChain details →

Each PentaGraph band filters the dry input on its own, and the results are summed.

Our approach

PentaGraph started with a recording of a real 5-band gyrator EQ pedal (48 kHz / 24-bit through a MOTU interface), analysed by spectral division with 1/6-octave smoothing.

Both plugins report zero latency. DecayChain's Legacy mode skips anti-aliasing on purpose, for a crunchier lo-fi sound.
